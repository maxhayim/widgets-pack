# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming to everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and technically constructive.
- Keep discussion focused on the widgets and how people use them.
- Do not publish other people's private information, such as names, locations, photos, API keys, or mesh node details, without their permission.
- Respect the terms of the data services and radio stations the widgets use.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, contributions, or participants that violate these expectations.
